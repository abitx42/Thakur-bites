CAMPUS PRIVACY CHARTER · TCET MUMBAI

PRIVACY POLICY & DATA GOVERNANCE

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 · Governing Entity: Thakur College of Engineering & Technology Canteen Committee

1. Purpose & Principles

Thakur Bites is an institutional food ordering and real-time kitchen dispatch system developed for students, faculty, and canteen staff at Thakur College of Engineering & Technology (TCET), Mumbai. We operate on a strict Zero-Surveillance and PII Minimization principle: we collect only the minimal technical data required to prepare, verify, and deliver your canteen orders.

2. Data We Collect

  • Institutional Identity: Your college email address (e.g., @tcetmumbai.in) and name, authenticated securely via Google Identity Platform.
  • Contact for OTP: Mobile phone number, used exclusively for Firebase Phone OTP verification.
  • Order History: Dish items, quantities, transaction timestamps, pickup tokens (e.g., T-104), and fulfillment state.
  • Preferences: Voluntary dietary selections (Jain, No Onion, Spicy) provided in the cart customizer.

3. Payment Security & PCI-DSS Scope

Thakur Bites never stores or processes credit card numbers, debit card PINs, UPI MPINs, or bank account credentials on college servers. All online monetary transactions are handled directly through Razorpay's RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant secure payment gateway.

4. Zero Advertising & Third-Party Non-Sharing

We do not display commercial advertisements. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose student or faculty information to third-party data brokers or marketing firms under any circumstances.

5. Data Deletion & Student Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and campus guidelines, any student or faculty member may request full account deletion and purging of personal records by contacting the TCET Canteen Committee or the system administrator.
