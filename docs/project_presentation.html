    THE 30-MINUTE RECESS CRUNCH

    Why generic food delivery apps fail in collegiate environments
    • Massive Surge Contention: 1,000+ students descend on the counter in a tight 30–45 minute lunch interval, causing 350% queue spikes.
    • Queue Starvation: Students buying a ₹15 beverage wait trapped behind multi-dish hot cooking queues.
    • Client-Side Pricing Exploits: Web ordering forms that calculate totals on the client can be tampered via DevTools (altering prices to ₹0).
    • Un-auditable Financial Discrepancies: Mixed cash counter and UPI transactions lack an immutable double-entry ledger.
    • AI Hallucinations: Standard LLMs connected to raw databases invent profit margins without raw ingredient procurement records.

    Financial Integrity Integer-Paise Double-Entry Bookkeeping

    MATHEMATICAL FINANCIAL INTEGRITY

    Eliminating floating-point drift and guaranteeing auditability
    • Integer-Paise Representation: ₹1 is stored as 100 paise; eliminates IEEE 754 floating-point rounding errors across checkout totals.
    • Double-Entry Ledger: Every payment capture automatically writes balanced debits and credits to `financialLedger`.
    • Deterministic Idempotency: Webhook retries and POS cash captures use deterministic keys (`cash_fin_${orderId}`) to block duplicate billing.
    • Autonomous Circuit Breakers: 3 levels of automated fault mitigation: Warning, Restricted Checkout, and Emergency Freeze.
